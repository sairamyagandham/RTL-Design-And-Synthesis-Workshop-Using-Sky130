# Day 02 – Timing Libraries, Hierarchical Design & Flip-Flops

## 📌 Overview

This repository contains my Day 2 learning and practical work from the **SKY130 RTL Design and Synthesis Workshop**.

The focus of Day 2 is on understanding timing libraries, hierarchical versus flat design, and various flip-flop concepts with RTL coding and synthesis experiments.

## 📚 Topics Covered

### 1. Timing Libraries
- Introduction to timing libraries.
- Understanding standard-cell libraries.
- Exploring the SKY130 standard-cell library.
- Understanding the importance of timing information in digital design.

### 2. Hierarchical vs. Flat Design
- Introduction to hierarchical design.
- Understanding flat design.
- Working with multiple Verilog modules.
- Module instantiation and submodule connections.
- Understanding hierarchical RTL synthesis.
- Exploring multi-module designs.

### 3. Various Flip-Flop Concepts
- Why flip-flops are required.
- Understanding different flip-flop configurations.
- D Flip-Flop with asynchronous reset.
- D Flip-Flop with synchronous reset.
- RTL coding and simulation of flip-flops.
- Flip-flop synthesis and standard-cell mapping.

## 🧪 Practical Experiments

The following designs and experiments were explored:

- D Flip-Flop with Asynchronous Reset
- D Flip-Flop with Synchronous Reset
- Flip-Flop RTL Simulation
- Flip-Flop Synthesis
- Multiple Module Verilog Design
- Submodule Instantiation
- 8-bit Multiplier Design
- Hierarchical RTL Synthesis

- **Waveform Viewer:** GTKWave

## 🎯 Key Learnings

- Understood the role of timing libraries in digital design.
- Learned the difference between hierarchical and flat design.
- Practiced designing multiple modules using Verilog.
- Understood synchronous and asynchronous reset operations.
- Explored RTL synthesis of different flip-flop designs.
- Observed the mapping of RTL designs into standard cells.
